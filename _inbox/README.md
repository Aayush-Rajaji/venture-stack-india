# _inbox

Raw material lands here. The AI reads it, extracts what matters into `stack/`, and the originals stay put as the audit trail.

**Nothing here is committed to git.** It routinely contains customer names, pricing, contracts, personal data and things people told you in confidence. See [docs/safety.md](../docs/safety.md).

---

## What to put in

Do not sort it. Do not clean it. Sorting is the AI's job.

| | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Call and meeting transcripts** | The highest-value input by a distance. What people actually said, not what a deck claims. Record every customer call, supplier call and mentor session. |
| Pitch decks — current and every dead version | The dead ones show what you stopped believing |
| Business plan, financial model, any spreadsheet with real numbers | |
| Market research and deep-research reports | The PDFs you commissioned and never re-read |
| Competitive intelligence | Screenshots, price lists, competitor sites, the WhatsApp tip-off about a new entrant |
| Customer conversations | Emails, WhatsApp exports, support threads, interview notes |
| Supplier quotes, invoices, POs, rate cards | The only honest source of what things actually cost |
| Website export or URL | Your public claims, which have to match your private ones |
| Registration and legal documents | Incorporation, GST, trademark, shareholding, signed agreements |
| Brand and design references | Logo files, colour codes, fonts, screenshots of pages you like |

## Two habits worth the extra minute

**Rescue the pictures.** A chart inside a PowerPoint or a PDF is invisible to text extraction — the AI reads the caption and misses the numbers *in* the graph. If a chart matters, export it as an image, drop the image in here alongside the file, and ask the AI to read the picture.

**Prefer plain text where it is free.** `.md` and `.txt` are lighter and cheaper for the AI than `.docx` and `.pdf`. Do not spend an hour converting things — Word files are fine as they are — but if something is already text, keep it text.

## Naming

Helpful but not required. If you can, prefix with a date and say what it is:

```
2024-05-12-call-transcript-distributor.md
2024-04-supplier-quote-packaging.pdf
pitch-deck-v4.pdf
pitch-deck-v1-DEAD.pdf
market-research-category.pdf
```

## Read-only

Once processed, leave files here. Do not delete them, and do not let the AI edit them — they are the evidence behind every citation in `stack/`. When someone challenges a number in eight months, this folder is what settles it.
