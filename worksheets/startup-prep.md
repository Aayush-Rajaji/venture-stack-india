# Startup Prep Framework

> **Purpose:** prepare for a 1:1 with a coach, mentor or investor by answering the essentials and planning concrete next steps.
> **Timebox: 45–60 minutes.** Bring your best guesses — evidence beats opinion, and a quick test beats a long debate.
> **Where it goes:** the answers here map directly onto `stack/` sections. Fill this in first if the full stack feels like too much.
