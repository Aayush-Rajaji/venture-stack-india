## The two you probably have not profiled

**The incumbent doing it badly.** Often large, sometimes government or heavily subsidised, product genuinely worse than yours — and given away free or near-free. Free-and-bad beats good-and-paid more often than founders expect, and you cannot out-price free. Work out what makes someone pay anyway.

**The adjacent platform.** The bigger player one step away who could add what you do in a quarter, to an audience they already have. Especially acute in software, where large AI ecosystems now absorb narrow use cases quickly. If your entire product could be a feature inside something your customers already use, that is worth knowing now — and it is a reason some founders are better served building something a platform cannot absorb.
