# Customer

> The section that everything else depends on and that is most often filled in from imagination. The test for this file is simple: **how many of these claims came from a conversation with a real person, and how many came from you thinking hard?** Both are allowed. Only one of them may be tagged `source-of-truth`.

Not a segment. A person, in a situation.

If you have more than one customer type, write each one separately and mark which is primary. The most expensive early mistake is trying to serve three customers with one message.
