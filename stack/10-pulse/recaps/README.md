## Append-only

Recaps are **never edited, corrected or consolidated after the fact.**

If a number in last week's recap turns out to have been wrong, note the correction in *this* week's recap. Do not go back and fix it.

The archive is a record of what you believed at the time, not a record of what turned out to be true. That is what makes it useful: reading twelve recaps in a row shows you whether your judgment is improving — whether the things you predicted happened, whether the reasons you gave for missing a target were real. A tidied archive tells you nothing, because a tidied archive was written by someone who already knew the answer.

## Do not skip the bad weeks

The pattern that kills companies is not a bad month. It is four bad months, each narrated as "building momentum," so that nobody — including the founder — notices in time.

A recap that says *three of five metrics went sideways and I do not yet know why* is doing exactly its job.
