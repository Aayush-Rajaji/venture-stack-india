## At risk

Not a ranking of who is behind. A short list of companies where something has changed and nobody has acted on it.

| Company | Signal | Since | What it needs | Who |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `[TBD]` | | | | |

Signals worth flagging, all of them observable from the record rather than from a feeling:

- **Gone quiet.** No session and nothing sent for 60 days. Usually not a documentation problem.
- **Stalled.** No rubric movement in two quarters on the function that was named as the focus.
- **Contradiction.** Two coaches have given advice that cannot both be followed. This is a programme failure, not a founder one, and it is fixed by the pre-session brief.
- **Reversed.** A level that went down. Rare and always worth a conversation — it usually means the earlier score was generous.
- **About to hit something.** A raise, a large order, a regulatory step, a hire — with the relevant function at 1 or 2.
