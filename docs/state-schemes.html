Up to Rs.1 Crore, plus 100% rental subsidy for workstations for 30 employees for one year in notified co-working/neighborhood-work/MSME parks

Eligibility: Deeptech and advanced technology startups approved under APISP

100% reimbursement of Net SGST accrued to the state from the startup for 5 years

Eligibility: Eligible startups under APISP (capped at 100% of eligible fixed capital investment)

8% of term loan due reimbursed

Eligibility: Eligible startups under APISP with a term loan

50% of patent expenses, up to Rs.2 Lakh for an India patent and Rs.10 Lakh for a foreign patent

Eligibility: Eligible startups under APISP that have filed patents
Reimbursement

Marketing / Fairs & Events Support

75% of cost, up to Rs.3 Lakhs (max 1 event per year per startup)

Eligibility: Eligible startups under APISP with an invitation/registration confirmation from the event organizer
Reimbursement

Accelerator Program Support

50% of program fee, up to Rs.2 Lakhs per program (maximum 2 programs per startup)

Eligibility: Eligible startups under APISP
Incubation

Matching Grant for Incubators/CoEs/Accelerators

Up to 100% matching grant under any Government of India scheme, limited to Rs.50 Lakhs

Eligibility: Select new or existing incubators/CoEs/accelerators in government universities or educational institutes
